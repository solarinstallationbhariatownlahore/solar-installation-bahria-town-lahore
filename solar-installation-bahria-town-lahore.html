Before You Decide

Planning, Permissions and Net Metering

System planning. Every installation starts with an assessment of your property and your electricity use. We plan panels, inverter and batteries as one system — not as separate purchases.

Community rules. Bahria Town has its own community guidelines for properties. We discuss any relevant considerations for your home during the consultation, and we plan installations that respect the community's standards.

Net metering. Where net metering is available, an on-grid or hybrid system can export surplus daytime energy to the grid. Approval and metering arrangements depend on your electricity provider and the applicable rules — we explain the process and what it involves, and we do not promise approvals or timelines we cannot control.

Frequently Asked Questions — Bahria Town Lahore

The cost depends on the size of the system, the type of system (hybrid, on-grid or off-grid), the panels, inverter and batteries selected, and the condition of the property and roof. Because every home and business is different, there is no single fixed price. Contact us for a free consultation and we will discuss your requirements and prepare a plan for your property.

There is no one-size-fits-all answer for a 5 Marla house. The right system depends on your electricity usage, the appliances you run, your roof space and whether you want battery backup. During a consultation we review your load and usage pattern and then suggest a system size that fits your home. Contact us on WhatsApp or by phone to discuss your 5 Marla home.

A 10 Marla house usually has more rooms and appliances, so the system is planned around your actual load — air conditioning, water motors, kitchen appliances and lighting all matter. We assess your property and energy needs first, then recommend a hybrid, on-grid or off-grid configuration that suits your home. A consultation is the right starting point.

An on-grid solar system is connected to the electricity grid. It generates solar power during the day and can export extra energy to the grid where net metering is available. It does not store energy, so it does not provide power during a grid outage on its own. A hybrid system combines solar panels with an inverter and battery storage, so it can store energy and keep essential loads running during outages, depending on the battery capacity and the loads connected.

The number of panels depends on your daily electricity consumption, the capacity of the panels chosen, and the usable roof space available. A small home with modest usage needs fewer panels than a larger home with heavy appliances. We calculate this from your load and roof assessment rather than guessing a number in advance.

Solar batteries store the energy your panels generate during the day so it can be used in the evening or during a power outage. They are a core part of hybrid and off-grid systems. Battery size and type are chosen according to how much backup you need and which appliances you want to run from stored energy.
